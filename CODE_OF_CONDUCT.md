# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in nuvx to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, or sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every nuvx space: the repository, issues, pull requests and the Discord server. It also applies when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to bxanee@gmail.com. Every report will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy respected. Maintainers may remove content or warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code.
