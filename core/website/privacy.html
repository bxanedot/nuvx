nuvx / PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the nuvx website, web player and browser extension, Android apps (including Android TV and Wear OS), the iOS source port, and desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux.

nuvx does not have its own user accounts.

When you connect a music or social service, nuvx uses the session or credentials needed for the feature you choose. Requests to those services are generally made directly from your device. While an app or web player is open, it automatically sends a random installation identifier and open status to the nuvx service for aggregate live counts. If you use optional Listen Together, the service also receives party identifiers, participant details, and music and playback information.

1. Who operates nuvx

nuvx is an independent software project maintained by bxane. Information is used to provide features you request, keep the software and services working, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. For privacy questions or requests, contact bxanee@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, session cookies, or other secrets by email.

2. Information stored on your device

The apps store the information needed to work, such as settings, selected accounts and profiles, playlists or queues, playback state, caches, lyrics preferences, and files you choose to download. The web player stores settings and presence identifiers in browser storage. Downloads and caches remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app data.

If you connect services, nuvx stores or uses the relevant session material locally: for example, YouTube cookies, Spotify session data, or optional Discord, Last.fm, Libre.fm, ListenBrainz, WebDAV or SMB credentials. Storage protection depends on the platform. Android uses encrypted preferences when available but can fall back to app-private preferences if encryption is unavailable. Desktop apps use the operating system credential store where supported and may use local app storage as a fallback. The browser extension stores its preferences and Spotify player data in browser extension storage; it reads YouTube or Spotify cookies only for the connection you enable.

The iOS port uses Apple Keychain and app storage; optional iCloud synchronization depends on your signing profile and settings. Sideloading tools handle your Apple Account separately to sign and refresh the app. nuvx does not collect your Apple Account password for installation. Consult the signing tool's privacy notice before using it.

To remove local data, disconnect the service in nuvx and clear the app or browser storage using that platform’s controls. Removing nuvx does not necessarily delete files you saved outside the app’s private storage.

3. Music services and other providers

When you search, browse, play, import a playlist, or request lyrics, the relevant query and content details are sent to the service or source needed for that feature. Depending on the app and feature, these can include YouTube Music or Google, Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, lyrics providers such as LRCLIB, Musixmatch, Genius, Better Lyrics, Unison, BiniLyrics and lrc.red, and sources you add yourself. Those services receive your network address and request metadata and handle information under their own privacy notices and terms. nuvx does not receive your music-service password.

Lyrics and translation requests can include song title, artist, album, language, duration, or lyrics text so the requested result can be found or translated. The exact provider depends on your settings and the feature you use. Custom source scripts and URLs are supplied by third parties or configured by you; their operators may receive the requests you send through them.

If you enable scrobbling, track details such as artist, title, album, duration and play time are sent to the service you select, such as Last.fm, Libre.fm or ListenBrainz. If you enable Discord rich presence, the current track and related display details are sent to Discord (or to the Discord client on desktop) so your status can be shown. You can disconnect or turn off these integrations in their settings.

4. Listen Together and live presence

When you join a Listen Together party, nuvx sends party information to its Cloudflare Worker service. This includes a random device identifier, a party member identifier, the display name and optional avatar URL you choose or provide, the party code, track identifiers and metadata, queue contents, playback state and position, and controls needed to synchronise participants. Party members can see the participant details, queue and playback information shared in that party. Audio is streamed separately by each participant and is not relayed by the nuvx party server.

Party state is temporary. Disconnected members are removed after about 45 seconds; an empty party is deleted after two minutes, and a party is deleted no later than 12 hours after it was created. Leave a party to stop sharing new party updates.

While an app or the web player is open, it also sends a random installation identifier, platform type and open/closed status to the same service so nuvx can show aggregate online counts. This runs automatically; it is not a music listening history. To limit fabricated counts, the service computes a SHA-256 hash of the request IP and counts at most five active installation identifiers for each hash; the raw IP is not stored in the presence table. Stale entries are considered offline after roughly six and a half minutes and are removed on the next stats query or open ping, or sooner when the service receives a close signal. The public counters show totals, not installation identifiers.

Cloudflare processes network requests and may handle IP addresses and technical request information for delivery, abuse prevention and security. The nuvx worker code does not intentionally keep request logs, but Cloudflare’s infrastructure may process or retain operational logs under its own terms.

5. Website, updates, and optional uploads

The nuvx project website is a static site. It does not intentionally use advertising or analytics scripts. A chosen colour theme is stored in your browser. The site may request public release or changelog information from GitHub and aggregate project counts from the nuvx service. Vercel hosts the project website; Cloudflare hosts the Listen Together service. These providers receive standard connection information, such as IP address, requested page or endpoint, time, browser or device details, and security data, as part of operating their services.

Apps may check GitHub for release or update information. GitHub receives those requests and their normal network metadata. The web player and extension are distributed through their respective hosting platforms; after publication, extension updates are delivered by the browser’s extension store. GitHub provides the project source code and is not the automatic update channel for store-installed extensions.

If you use the optional canvas upload service, the video file and its YouTube Music video identifier are sent to the nuvx upload worker and then added to the public nuvx GitHub repository. Uploaded files are publicly accessible and may be copied or indexed by others. Do not upload content unless you are comfortable making it public and have the right to share it.

6. Retention, security, and choices

Settings, credentials, caches, queues and downloads are kept locally until you clear them. nuvx does not operate a central music account database. Listen Together party and presence data follow the short retention periods described above. A canvas upload remains public in the repository until it is removed. Third-party providers set their own retention periods for the requests they receive.

Use the app’s disconnect controls to stop using a service, leave Listen Together to stop sharing party state, disable scrobbling or rich presence if enabled, and revoke optional browser permissions in your browser settings. You can clear locally stored app or browser data through the platform. If you need help with data handled by nuvx-operated services, contact bxanee@gmail.com with the feature and approximate time involved; do not include secrets.

nuvx uses platform storage protections and encrypted connections where available, but no software or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Each music, social, hosting, or source provider has its own security and privacy practices.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or limits on personal information, or to object to certain processing. You can also contact the relevant privacy regulator. Contact nuvx at bxanee@gmail.com for requests about information handled by the project. For data held by YouTube, Google, Spotify, Discord, GitHub, Cloudflare, Vercel, or another provider, use that provider’s own privacy controls and contact process.

8. Children and changes

nuvx is general-purpose software and is not designed to collect information from children. The project may update this notice when the software or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
